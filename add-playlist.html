How to add playlist

1.
Open a search engine (e.g. Google)
2.
Search for IPTV Playlist ↗
3.
Open any result and copy a playlist link (ending with .m3u or .m3u8)
https://example.com/index.m3u
4.
Switch back to the app, tap “Add Playlist”, choose “From URL”, and paste the link
5.
Enjoy watching!
⌕ Search in browser
⚠ Note

Some channels in public playlists may be unavailable. This is not an app issue — just try another channel.
