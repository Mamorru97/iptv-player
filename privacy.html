Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

IPTV Player ("we", "our", or "us") operates the IPTV Player mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require users to create an account, and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or payment card details.

However, the app and third-party services used by the app may collect limited technical, analytics, and purchase-related information as described below.

  • App usage data, such as screens visited, interactions, and feature usage
  • Device information, such as device type, operating system version, app version, and language
  • Approximate location, such as country or region
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash reports and performance information
  • Purchase and subscription information, such as product identifiers, subscription status, purchase status, renewal status, and receipt-related information

This information is used to operate the app, provide premium features, restore purchases, improve app performance, understand feature usage, and maintain app reliability.

2. Local User Data

User-provided playlist data, playlist URLs, cached playlist data, favorites, recently watched channels, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not operate IPTV content servers and we do not upload your playlist contents to our own servers.

If you delete the app, locally stored app data may also be deleted from your device, depending on your device settings and operating system behavior.

3. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

The app offers optional in-app purchases, including premium access and subscriptions. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment card number, billing address, or other payment card details.

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases, subscriptions, purchase validation, entitlements, and purchase restoration. RevenueCat may process information such as an anonymous app user identifier, product identifiers, purchase status, subscription status, receipt information, and related technical data.

This information is used to determine whether premium features should be unlocked, to restore purchases, to prevent purchase errors, and to keep subscription status accurate.

4. Analytics and Diagnostic Data

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the app and to improve the user experience. Analytics data may include app usage events, device information, approximate location, and diagnostic information.

Analytics data is used in aggregated or non-directly identifying form whenever possible.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Apple App Store and In-App Purchases
  • RevenueCat
  • Google Firebase Analytics

You can learn more about their privacy practices here:

  • https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The app does not use website cookies. However, third-party services used by the app may use identifiers, SDKs, or similar technologies to provide analytics, diagnostics, purchase validation, and subscription management.

We do not use this information to sell your personal data.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with third-party service providers only as necessary to operate the app, process purchases, manage subscriptions, restore purchases, analyze app usage, improve performance, and comply with legal requirements.

8. Data Retention

Locally stored app data remains on your device unless you delete it, reset the app, or uninstall the app.

Purchase and subscription-related information may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat as necessary to provide purchase history, subscription validation, entitlement management, refund handling, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and purchase restoration.

Analytics and diagnostic data may be retained by third-party providers according to their own retention policies.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will take appropriate steps to delete such information if required.

11. Your Choices

You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID settings. You can also use the Restore Purchases option in the app to restore previously purchased access.

You can delete locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
